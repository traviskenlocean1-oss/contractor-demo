General Construction in Jacksonville, FL

El Duro Construction has the crew and experience to take on projects beyond individual trade work. From framing and structural builds to full interior renovations, we manage the scope and deliver quality craftsmanship on schedule.

Jacksonville's construction market moves fast. Having a reliable contractor who shows up, communicates clearly, and does the work themselves — without subcontracting the critical parts — is rare. That's what sets El Duro apart on larger scopes.

Our general construction work spans residential and light commercial projects. We take on room additions, garage conversions, structural repairs, interior gut-and-rebuild scopes, and custom build projects that need consistent management from start to finish.
