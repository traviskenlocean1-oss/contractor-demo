Hardwood Flooring in Jacksonville, FL

Hardwood flooring adds warmth, value, and timeless appeal to any home. But Florida's humidity and temperature swings make proper installation critical — wood that isn't acclimated correctly will expand, buckle, or gap after installation.

At El Duro, we understand Florida's climate. Every hardwood job starts with the right acclimation period and a thorough subfloor assessment. We match the installation method to the species, subfloor type, and room conditions — so your floor performs the way it's supposed to.

What Makes a Good Hardwood Install

Florida's humidity ranges widely throughout the year. Wood needs to acclimate in your home's conditioned environment — usually 5–10 days — before installation begins. This stabilizes the moisture content so the boards expand and contract predictably after they're installed.

Subfloor prep is equally critical. We check for flatness (no more than 3/16" over 10 feet), moisture levels, and structural integrity before a single board goes down. The installation method — nail-down, glue-down, or floating — depends on the species and subfloor type.

🌡

Proper Acclimation

Wood boxes are staged in the install space for 5–10 days so the wood adjusts to your home's humidity and temperature before any nailing begins.

📐

Subfloor Assessment

We test moisture levels and flatness before installation. High moisture or an uneven subfloor are corrected first — not ignored.

🔨

Right Install Method

Nail-down, glue-down, or floating — each method has specific applications. We use the right one for your species, subfloor, and room conditions.
