Hardwood Flooring Near You? Contact El Duro Today!

Hardwood flooring is one of the most impactful upgrades you can make to your home. It adds warmth, character, and real resale value — but only when it's installed correctly by a crew that knows what they're doing.

At El Duro, we handle every step from subfloor assessment and prep to installation and finishing. We don't rush the process because proper hardwood installation requires precision at every stage.

1

Subfloor Assessment & Prep

We assess your existing subfloor for levelness, moisture, and structural integrity before any boards go down — this is what separates lasting floors from floors that fail.

2

Precision Installation

Boards are acclimated to your space, then installed with proper spacing, staggered joints, and the correct fastening method for your subfloor type and species choice.

3

Finishing & Final Details

We install trim, transitions, and base molding for a complete, professional look — not just the floor itself, but everything that makes it look finished.
