1

Proper Grading for Drainage

Every sidewalk we build is graded for proper water runoff — preventing puddling, surface erosion, and long-term deterioration in Florida's rainy season.

Code-Compliant Construction

We build to local Jacksonville and Northeast Florida codes — correct widths, slopes, control joints, and edge treatment to meet municipal and ADA standards where required.

How It Works

Our Sidewalk Process

From your first call to a finished walkway — here's exactly what to expect when you work with El Duro.

01

Free Estimate

We visit your property, measure the scope, and give you a clear written price.

02

Excavate & Grade

We excavate to depth, compact the base, and set forms to the correct grade for drainage.

03

Pour & Finish

Concrete is poured, screeded level, and finished with a broom texture for safe traction.

04

Cut Joints & Complete

Control joints are cut to prevent cracking, and we walk through the finished work with you.
