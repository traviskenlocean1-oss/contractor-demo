FAQ

Common
Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary by scope. Residential renovations typically take 3–6 months, custom home builds 12–18 months, and commercial projects 18–36 months. We provide detailed schedules at consultation with weekly milestone updates throughout.

How do you ensure projects stay within budget?

We use fixed-price contracts with detailed scope definitions. Our in-house cost estimators use real-time material pricing databases, and we maintain a 5% contingency reserve. Any change orders are approved by you before execution — no surprises.

What quality standards do you follow?

All projects comply with local building codes, IBC standards, and OSHA regulations. We hold LEED certification, ISO 9001 quality management certification, and perform third-party inspections at every major milestone.

What sectors do you specialize in?

We operate across residential luxury, commercial office, retail, industrial/manufacturing, hospitality, and mixed-use developments. Our largest sector is commercial, representing 60% of our annual project volume.

What safety measures are in place?

Safety is non-negotiable. We maintain an EMR of 0.68, well below the industry average of 1.0. All workers complete 30-hour OSHA training, and we conduct weekly safety audits on every active site.

What contract options are available?

We offer fixed-price contracts, cost-plus with GMP (Guaranteed Maximum Price), and design-build contracts. All include milestone payment schedules, performance bonds, and 1-year workmanship warranties on all completed work.
