Concrete Contractor Near You? Contact El Duro Today!

Choosing the right concrete contractor is one of the most important decisions for your property. A properly poured and finished concrete surface can last decades — done poorly, it cracks, sinks, or deteriorates within years.

At El Duro, we handle every step ourselves — from excavation and forming to the pour and finish. We don't subcontract the critical work, which means you get consistent quality on every job.

1

Proper Subbase Preparation

We excavate to the correct depth and compact the base material — this prevents settling and cracking over time and cannot be skipped on quality work.

2

Steel Reinforcement Every Time

Rebar or wire mesh is placed before every pour to handle tensile stress and prevent cracking under load or ground movement.

3

Managed Curing for Maximum Strength

Concrete gains strength over 28 days. We manage the curing process to ensure your slab reaches full design strength for Florida's climate.
