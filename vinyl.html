Vinyl & LVP Flooring Near You? Contact El Duro Today!

Luxury Vinyl Plank has become the top flooring choice for Florida homeowners — and for good reason. It's 100% waterproof, highly durable, warmer underfoot than tile, and available in styles that convincingly mimic hardwood or stone.

At El Duro, we handle every step from subfloor prep to final trim. Proper LVP installation starts with a flat, clean subfloor — imperfections show through, and we do that prep work before the first plank ever goes down.

1

Subfloor Assessment & Leveling

We assess and flatten the subfloor before installation — most floating LVP products require a surface within 3/16" flatness over 10 feet. This step is what separates lasting floors from floors that fail.

2

Precision Floating Installation

LVP planks are installed with proper expansion gaps and staggered seams throughout. We select the right wear layer and plank thickness for your application — high-traffic and pet homes need a different spec than a guest room.

3

Trim & Transition Finishing

We install transitions, quarter-round molding, and thresholds at every door and wall for a fully finished, professional result — not just the floor itself, but everything that makes it look complete.
