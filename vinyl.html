Vinyl & LVP Flooring in Jacksonville, FL

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) has become the go-to flooring choice for Florida homeowners — and for good reason. It's 100% waterproof, highly durable, warmer underfoot than tile, and comes in styles that convincingly mimic hardwood, stone, and more.

At El Duro, we handle the full installation from subfloor prep to final trim. Proper LVP installation starts with a flat, clean subfloor — imperfections show through, and most floating floors require a surface within 3/16" flatness over 10 feet. We do that work before the first plank goes down.

Why LVP Works for Florida Homes

Florida's humidity is hard on natural materials. Solid hardwood can warp, tile grout can crack, and carpet holds moisture and allergens. LVP handles humidity swings without expanding or contracting significantly, making it one of the most practical flooring choices in the state.

Modern LVP products have wear layers ranging from 6 mil to 20+ mil. We help you select the right wear layer and plank thickness for your specific application — high-traffic areas and homes with pets need a different spec than a guest bedroom.

💧

100% Waterproof

Unlike hardwood or laminate, LVP won't swell, buckle, or delaminate when water hits it — ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms.

🐾

Pet & Kid Friendly

Scratch-resistant wear layers handle claws and foot traffic. Spills wipe up clean without worrying about staining or swelling.

🏗

Goes Over Most Subfloors

LVP can float over concrete, plywood, or existing tile (when the conditions are right), often eliminating the need for a full demo.
